A Tribute in Verse

✦ ✦ ✦

He built a home from dreams and sweat and stone,

A shelter made of sacrifice and grace—

He set aside the seeds he'd always sown

So we could find a warmer, brighter place.

His quiet hands worked long before the dawn,

And never once did he demand his due;

Behind each rule, a heart that carried on,

A heart of gold in everything he'd do.

Each storm he faced with shoulders broad and still,

Each mountain crossed to keep his family fed—

He gave us wings, though never asked he will,

And walked beside the paths where others fled.

Today we lift our voices, hearts, and eyes

To honour fifty years of love made true.

His legacy shines bright in every sunrise—

Papa, this world is richer — because of you.

— With all our love, always —
